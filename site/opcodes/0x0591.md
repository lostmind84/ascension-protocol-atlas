# `0x0591` SMSG_PATCH_CHALLENGE

**server to client** · status `hypothetical` · evidence `L3` · layout `named` · client code `handler`

Fiche: [`protocol/opcodes/0x0591-smsg-patch-challenge.yaml`](../../protocol/opcodes/0x0591-smsg-patch-challenge.yaml)

One row of Challenge.dbc in the compact form the wire uses: twenty-one dwords then six length-prefixed strings, written over the six dwords the reader skips (4, 7, 8, 10, 11 and 20). The runtime record is 0x54 bytes and dword-equal to the wire block. The client's own Challenge.dbc has 53 fields of 212 bytes, so the wire row is a projection of the file, not a copy of it -- the largest packet in the challenge patch family and still the least named: not one of the twenty-one dwords is pinned to a column.
