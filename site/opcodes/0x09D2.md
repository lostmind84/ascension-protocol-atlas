Fiche: [`protocol/opcodes/0x09d2-smsg-patch-occlusion-volume.yaml`](../../protocol/opcodes/0x09d2-smsg-patch-occlusion-volume.yaml)

One row of OcclusionVolume.dbc, written into the client's own copy of that table (patch-tables.yaml's CustomDBCMgr scan binds this opcode to it, and the decompile confirms the idiom: the row goes into the table object's rows array at (id - minId), bounds-checked against minId/maxId, exactly as client/patch-tables.md describes). The wire's 16-byte fixed part is not a plain 4-dword block copy: the client reads only 3 of its 4 dwords into named values (offsets 0x00, 0x08, 0x0c; the dword at 0x04 is never read into a value at all) and then, at store time, places the trailing cstring's pointer into that 4th row slot instead -- exactly the "string stitched over the record's string-offset column" pattern client/patch-tables.md describes, just with the stitched column in the middle of the row rather than at its end. No source in this repository names any of the table's 4 columns: checked handler-strings.yaml, lua-keys.yaml and the UI corpus (see naming_source below).
