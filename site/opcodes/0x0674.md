All addon-side auras of a unit: the unit's guid then 28-byte aura records to the end of the packet. The record is the capture's, whose sizes divide exactly (8 + n * 28 over six packets); our decompilation of FUN_10a12470 saw four u32 on one branch and three on another, which is the same record read badly.
