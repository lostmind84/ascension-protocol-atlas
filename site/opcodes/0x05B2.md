One row of ChallengeConditions.dbc, patched into the client's table: ten dwords then the ConditionType token as a length-prefixed string. The runtime record is dword-equal to the wire block (0x28 bytes) with the string pointer written over dword 5, which the reader skips. Six of the ten dwords are named: the row key, ElseGroup, ConditionValue1-3 and NegativeCondition, from the offsets the detailedChallenge condition builder at 0x1013c7de reads.
