Sent by the Lua binding C_RecoveryService.RecoverCategoryItemAtIndex(category, index, id) (FUN_10306a10) when the category string maps to table index 2, RECOVERY_SERVICE_CATEGORY_DELETED_CHARACTER. Two fields: a u32 position and the id string, NUL-terminated. The position is the index of the entry in the client's cached list for the category (the binding turns the Lua index into it through a lookup in that list and refuses the call when the entry is not found).
