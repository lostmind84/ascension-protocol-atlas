Fiche: [`protocol/opcodes/0x09bf-smsg-seasonal-collection-unlocked.yaml`](../../protocol/opcodes/0x09bf-smsg-seasonal-collection-unlocked.yaml)

Seasonal collection unlocked: no packet read is visible in the handler, and none is needed. `handler_0x09bf` takes no field from the `CDataStore` at all; it calls `FUN_10310320()` -- a lazily-constructed singleton whose vftable Ghidra itself names `SeasonalCollectionMgr::vftable` -- and sets the byte at `+0xa0` on it to 1. The mere arrival of the opcode is the whole payload. This reads as a durable one-shot unlock flag, not a report of *what* unlocked: the Lua-exposed getter `IsSeasonalCollectionUnlocked` (bound at the very same address the handler jumps through, RVA 0x003111d0) reads that identical `+0xa0` byte and returns it to Lua as a boolean, so the handler and the getter are provably reading and writing the same field, cross-checked two ways in this build. A companion opcode, 0x09be (handler RVA 0x10310430, not ours), sits right before it and is not examined here.
