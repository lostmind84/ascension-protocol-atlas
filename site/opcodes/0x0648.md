# `0x0648` SMSG_SKILL_CARD_COLLECTION_LIST

**server to client** · status `hypothetical` · evidence `L1` · layout `named` · client code `handler`

Fiche: [`protocol/opcodes/0x0648-smsg-skill-card-collection-list.yaml`](../../protocol/opcodes/0x0648-smsg-skill-card-collection-list.yaml)

The skill-card collection: a u32, a 96-byte block of 24 u32 copied into the skill-card service, a count, then that many 12-byte entries. Byte-exact against the capture reference's 440-byte payload (4 + 96 + 4 + 28 x 12); what the crosscheck called a conflict was its own reading of their struct[28] as a 28-byte record, where they mean 28 records of 12 bytes.
